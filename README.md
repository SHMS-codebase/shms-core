# 🏥 SHMS — Smart Healthcare Management System

SHMS is a secure, web-based healthcare management platform built with Spring Boot and Thymeleaf. It streamlines clinical and administrative workflows for hospitals and clinics, offering audit-ready features, responsive design, and modular architecture.

---

## 📌 Project Scope

The Smart Healthcare Management System provides a comprehensive platform for healthcare facilities to efficiently manage their operations. The following modules are included:

### 🔍 In Scope

- **Doctor Profile Management**  
  Track credentials, specialties, and availability.

- **Patient Record Management with EHR Capabilities**  
  Maintain secure, structured electronic health records.

- **Appointment Scheduling and Calendar Management**  
  Enable patients and staff to book, view, and manage appointments.

- **Treatment Plan Creation and Medication Tracking**  
  Document treatment protocols and associated prescriptions.

- **Billing and Payment Processing**  
  Generate invoices, process payments, and manage receipts.

- **Analytical Reports and Dashboard Generation**  
  Visualize key metrics for operational and clinical insights.

- **User Authentication and Authorization**  
  Role-based access control for patients, doctors, and administrators.

- **Audit Logging and Compliance Reporting**  
  Maintain traceable logs for all critical actions to support regulatory audits.
